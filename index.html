Inside the app

A closer look inside the app.

Which Japan visa photo size should I choose?

Use the photo instructions for your visa type and the office handling your application. Check whether you’re attaching a photo to a form or uploading a file.

Singapore · paper sightseeing visa

The Japan Visa Application Centre (JVAC) checklist for single- or double-entry sightseeing asks for a 35×45 mm unedited photo on a white background. Glue it to the form. The checklist rejects regular A4 printouts. Take a new original against a white background or use a photo studio for this route.

The embassy directs long-term Singapore residents who need a visa to JVAC. Singapore citizens are visa-exempt for temporary visits of up to 90 days.

JVAC sightseeing checklist (PDF) · Embassy instructions

Singapore · online tourist eVISA

For eligible tourist eVISA applicants, the embassy links to the separate JVAC online route. Follow that route’s current digital upload instructions before choosing a crop.

Singapore JVAC eVISA · Japan Ministry of Foreign Affairs eVISA guidance

Melbourne · paper short-stay visa

The consulate lists 35×45 mm or 2×2 in for a photo attached to the form for tourism or visiting someone in Japan. It asks for a white background and bars editing filters.

Melbourne short-stay checklist

San Francisco · paper work or long-stay visa with a COE

After you receive your Certificate of Eligibility, the consulate’s checklist accepts a 2×2 in square or a 2×1.4 in portrait on glossy photo paper. It tells applicants to avoid apps that alter their facial image. The Ministry’s general paper form also labels a portrait photo box, 35 mm wide by 45 mm high.

San Francisco work and long-stay checklist · Ministry visa form (PDF)

What the app checks

  • Photo size — choose 35×45mm or U.S. passport 2×2in, then confirm the correct option with your consulate.
  • Head height — the app checks an estimated crown-to-chin measurement.
  • Plain white background — after automatic cutout, no shadows or patterns.
  • Recency & expression — the checklist reminds you the photo should be recent (within 6 months), neutral expression, no hat.
  • No face reshaping — segmentation and cropping only; we don't slim or resize facial geometry.
  • Privacy — segmentation and matting run on your iPhone; no separate upload to our servers for processing.
